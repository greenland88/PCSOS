# PCS annualized performance reporting

Replay summaries now include account performance and capital-efficiency metrics. `CAGR` uses starting and ending equity over the requested test period. `annualized_return_on_average_capital` uses total realized P&L divided by average collateral used; it does not annualize individual trades. PCS also reports planned-loss exposure separately when present.

## ACCOUNT PERFORMANCE

`CAGR`, ending equity, drawdown, profit factor, expectancy, win rate, and trade count.

## PCS CAPITAL EFFICIENCY

`annualized_return_on_average_capital` and the planned-loss-based annualized return. These are not CAGR.
